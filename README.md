# Tipoff

Every game in one place: basketball (NBA, WNBA, NCAA M, NCAA W), football (NFL, NCAA F) and baseball (MLB, NCAA B). Live at **https://raylikeray.github.io/tipoff/**

- **Layout:** a command center per league. Pick a sport in the header switch (Basketball · Football · Baseball), then a league in the tab strip under it, and the home screen shows everything about it at once: today's scoreboard rail, tonight's call (the featured game and why the model leans the way it does), standings or rankings, leaders, injuries with their line impact (or 7-day movers for college), power ratings, the model's record, and news. The section bar under the tabs opens the full pages: Scores, Standings, Rankings (college), Teams, Players, Forecasts, Injuries (NBA/WNBA), News. The model's full record (`#record`) is not a tab; it is linked from each center's Model module, the Forecasts page and the footer. Links are `#<league>/<section>`; old links still work.
- **My teams (`#my`):** the ★ tab at the front of the sport switch (and the logo, once teams are picked) opens one screen for every team you follow across every sport: live games now, a card per team (record, standing, next game with the forecast, last result, who's out) grouped by sport, up next with forecasts, results from the last seven days, and a wire of your teams' injuries and transactions. It is the landing page as soon as any team is picked. League tabs carry a ★ where you have teams.
- **Personal:** first visit opens a welcome picker (every league, grouped by sport) and an updates panel; later, "Pick my teams" on each league screen or the bell in the header reopen it. The picker is a logo grid (saved on the device only, no accounts). Your teams get a strip at the top with the next or live game, the forecast, the last result and who is out; their games go first in the scoreboard and their rows are highlighted. The site installs to the home screen (manifest + `sw.js`; an install strip appears once on phones). **Updates for my teams** (`js/app.js`, favorites only, while Tipoff is open or installed): pregame 30 minutes before tip with the forecast and who is out, close games late, finals, injury-report changes (checked every 15 min), and transactions (every 30 min). Each kind can be toggled; nothing is sent for other teams. True push with the app closed is `push/worker.js` (a free Cloudflare Worker; see `push/README.md`), switched on by setting `PUSH.url` in `js/config.js` once it is deployed. 
- **Sports:** `js/config.js` carries one entry per league (`sport`, ESPN path, periods, spread, home advantage, Elo speed, season start, which feeds exist). Football leagues are weekly: the center shows the current week's slate. Baseball games show the line score with R/H/E and outs and runners while live; football shows down and distance and who has the ball. Box scores render every stat category ESPN sends (passing/rushing/receiving…, batting/pitching). The Players page lists ESPN's season leaders by category for football and baseball (sortable), and per-game averages for basketball.
- **Watch:** the broadcaster on every scoreboard card, forecast row and game page is a link that opens the game where *you* watch. In the team picker, **Where I watch** lets a viewer tap the services they pay for (Sling TV, YouTube TV, Hulu + Live TV, Fubo, DirecTV Stream, ESPN app, Prime Video, Peacock, Paramount+, Max, Netflix, Apple TV, NBA/WNBA League Pass, NFL+, YouTube Sunday Ticket, MLB.TV) in priority order; a game's link goes to the first of those that carries its channel (an ESPN game opens Sling for a Sling subscriber), otherwise to the channel's own app. On Android the links are `intent://` URLs that launch the installed app and fall back to the web page; elsewhere they are the services' live pages. Each league center has a **Where to watch** module listing the day's games by service. Tables: `SERVICES`, `SERVICE_GROUPS`, `REGIONAL` in `js/core.js`; the choice is saved on the device (`hw:watch`).
- **Live data:** ESPN's public site API, fetched in the browser. Scores refresh every 30 s while games are live; the league tabs carry a live count.
- **Forecasts:** margin-based Elo per league, rebuilt nightly by `tools/nightly.mjs ratings` (GitHub Action `nightly.yml`) into `data/<league>/ratings.json`; the browser adds home court, rest and an injury adjustment weighted by scoring (`js/core.js`). Every pregame call is scored against the result and ESPN's own pregame win probability in `data/<league>/record.json`, shown on the Record page. Each call carries two lines: `line`, the closing line ESPN's summary keeps after the final, and `line0`, the line on the scoreboard the first time the job saw the game that day (the morning run, normally) with `at0` its UTC time — the number RIVL showed is the blend of its own margin with the line at the time, so the Record page scores the blend from `line0` when it is there, which is the conservative reading, and from the closing line otherwise. `gh`/`ga` are the games each side had played, for the early-season weight.
- **The model learns, a little:** `tools/nightly.mjs tune` runs after the ratings, at most once a day whatever triggered the job (the job also runs on pushes to the script; on 2026-10-29 seven runs in one afternoon took seven steps on the same 68 games, which is why the once-a-day guard exists and why the NBA parameters were reset to the defaults on 2026-10-30). Replayed walk-forward over the 2026 WNBA season, tuning scored 0.1963 against 0.1971 for the fixed defaults — a few ten-thousandths, within noise — so the tuner is kept for its calibration layer and the record it leaves, not as a source of accuracy. `tools/nightly.mjs rebuild` replays the season under the parameters in `params.json` without a step, for a reset. It It replays the whole season under a grid of home-court, rest, spread and rating-speed values, keeps the mix with the best Brier score, moves the live parameters part of the way there (capped per night so it learns gradually), fits a calibration layer once there is enough data, and rebuilds the ratings with the new mix. Results go to `data/<league>/params.json` with a history that the Record page shows as a tuning log; the section bar shows the latest change. Tuning starts after a league-specific number of games (`tuneAt` in `js/config.js`: 60 NBA, 40 WNBA, 150 college basketball and football, 100 NFL, 300 baseball); baseball tunes in tenths of a run. A league added mid-season is back-filled from its first game (ESPN's pregame numbers are only collected for the last 45 days).
- No build step. Edit `index.html`, `css/app.css`, `js/*.js` and push; bump the `?v=` on the asset links in `index.html` so browsers pick up changes.

## Design: Broadcast
Mark: a disc split by the line at center court, the top half already tipping off (`img/symbol.svg`; app icon `img/mark.svg`; PNG icons in `img/`). Two-tone: ink over orange on light, white over orange on dark.
Studio-gray ground, white panels, ink module headers with an angled orange tab, team abbreviations in their own color block, condensed numerals for scores and percentages, a red block for anything live. Barlow for text, Barlow Condensed for numbers and labels. Dark mode is the same system on near-black. All tokens are at the top of `css/app.css`; the 12-column module grid collapses to two columns on tablets and one on phones, where a sticky jump bar moves between modules.
